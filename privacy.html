Online Radio for Android

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Online Radio is an Android app that plays internet radio stations. It has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising, and it never sells or shares your data.

What the app stores

The app keeps these on your device, in its private app storage:

  • your favorite stations
  • the stations you played recently
  • custom stations you add (name, stream URL and location)
  • the volume level and the country you last browsed
  • your sleep timer and alarm settings
  • whether the Pro unlock has been purchased

This information stays on your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app deletes it. If Android backup is enabled on your device, it may be included in your device backup under Google's backup terms.

Network connections the app makes

  • Station streams. When you play a station, the app connects directly to that station's streaming server to receive the audio. The station's operator can see your IP address, as with any website you visit. Some stations only offer unencrypted (http://) streams; the app plays those as they are.
  • Station logos. Logos are loaded from the URLs that the stations publish, so the app connects to those servers to fetch the images.
  • Google Play Billing. The optional Pro unlock is a one-time purchase handled by Google Play. The app only asks Google Play whether the purchase has been made; it never sees your payment details. Google's handling of purchases is covered by the Google Play Terms of Service and Google's Privacy Policy.

The app makes no other network requests. The station catalogue is bundled with the app, so search and browsing work offline. The app does not load remote code.

Permissions

  • Internet plays streams and loads logos.
  • Foreground service (media playback) keeps the radio playing when the app is in the background, with a notification you can control it from.
  • Post notifications shows the playback notification.
  • Schedule exact alarms (optional) lets the Pro alarm start a station at the time you set. You can decline it; the alarm then rings when Android allows.
  • Receive boot completed re-registers a set alarm after the device restarts.
  • Wake lock keeps the network connection up while playing.

Station data

The station catalogue is derived from Radio Browser, a community-maintained directory. Station names, stream URLs and logos belong to their respective broadcasters. Country flags are from the open-source flag-icons project.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.
